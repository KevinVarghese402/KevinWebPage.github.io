Eclispe Reborn

Our Recent Project - Eclipse Reborn is a vertical scrolling, bullet hell roguelite. Shifting environments and floods of enemies, where players have to dodge attacks and projectiles. With the aid of upgrades to go ever further in the levels.

  • Worked as art director producing the player and enemy assets, animation, title page for the game
  • Was project manager - managing which systems should be worked on first and delegating tasks to prevent burnout
  • Made sure expectations were met with my self and team, and creating plans if expectations were not met

Reflection

Reflection is a story about an Indian man raised outside his mother country navigating the challenge of fitting in, as a cultrual event is around the corner. Make the right choices to see how the story plays and view mutliple ending.

  • Worked as art director producing the player game assets, animation
